How to Use the Concrete Calculator

Whether you are pouring a backyard patio, a sidewalk, or foundation footings, knowing the precise amount of concrete prevents costly over-ordering or frustrating project delays. Our calculator takes your linear measurements and automatically computes the volume required, factoring in a standard 5% spillage safety margin.

Standard Concrete Bag Requirements:

  • 1 cubic yard = 27 cubic feet
  • 80 lb bag yields approximately 0.60 cubic feet (approx. 45 bags per cu. yd.)
  • 60 lb bag yields approximately 0.45 cubic feet (approx. 60 bags per cu. yd.)
